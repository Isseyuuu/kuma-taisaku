Questions about bears in Japan

Where do bears live, and do species differ by region?

Species differ by region; check official sources for confirmed distribution information. The specific regional breakdown is not stated here because it has not yet been verified against official material.

Can bears appear in tourist areas?

Alert and sighting information can change. Check the local government and site manager for the exact destination before your visit.

How can I follow alerts without reading Japanese?

The Ministry of the Environment published an English bear-awareness leaflet for international visitors in September 2026. Its existence and publication date are confirmed; use the official PDF directly rather than relying on an unverified summary.

Items that make sound

Nagano Prefecture recommends making non-natural sounds with items such as a bear bell, radio, or whistle to make your presence known. Check that you have a sound-making item before entering the mountains.

Sound-making items are intended to make your presence known. They do not guarantee prevention of a bear encounter or injury. Carrying an item is only one part of the official guidance; follow the trail actions below and check current local information as well.

Where can I buy a bear bell?

This site has not verified where international visitors can purchase a bear bell in Japan. The confirmed point is the purpose of a bell, radio, or whistle: making your presence known.

Autumn and time of day

During autumn, from September through November, bears are more active in the pre-hibernation feeding period. Autumn is also a season when more people enter the mountains for recreation.

Early morning and evening are times when bear appearances are more likely. Consider whether your plan includes these times, and check current local notices before setting out.

Actions on the trail

Nagano Prefecture's guidance includes the following actions:

  • Avoid going alone.
  • Avoid activity close to dense vegetation as much as possible.
  • Act on the assumption that bears are present in the mountains.
  • If you find bear tracks or droppings, do not move closer. Turn back.

If you encounter a bear

The following actions are officially recommended by Nagano Prefecture.

  1. Keep your eyes on the bear.
  2. Move away slowly without provoking it.
  3. Do not turn your back and run. Bears have a tendency to chase things that flee.
  4. If escape is difficult, lie face down and protect your head and neck with your hands. This posture is recommended as a way to lessen injury.

Source: Nagano Prefecture, information about Asian black bears.

Check information before entering

Alerts can change. Check the latest information from the local government and site manager for your destination before entering the mountains.
