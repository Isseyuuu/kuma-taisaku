For Asian black bear areas

Before you go in, and once you are in

These points summarize Nagano Prefecture's guidance for Asian black bears. Their application to Hokkaido brown bears has not been verified.

Make your presence known

Nagano Prefecture recommends making non-natural sounds with items such as a bear bell, radio, or whistle. These items do not guarantee prevention of an encounter or injury.

Avoid going alone

Avoid solo activity and stay away from areas close to dense vegetation as much as possible.

Turn back at signs

If you find bear tracks or droppings, do not move closer. Turn back.

Questions international visitors may have

Where in Japan do bears live?

Compare the four main islands first. Regional distribution is not a live sighting report for a specific trail.

Can bears appear in tourist areas?

Check the local government and site manager for your destination before visiting. Alert and sighting information can change.

Where can I buy a bear bell in Japan?

Nagano Prefecture confirms that bear bells, radios, and whistles can be used to make your presence known. This site has not verified where international visitors can purchase them.

How can I check alerts if I do not read Japanese?

The Ministry of the Environment published an English bear-awareness leaflet for international visitors in September 2026. Use the official links below, and check current information from the local government or site manager for your destination.
